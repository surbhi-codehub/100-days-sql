# 🎯 Today's SQL Missions

### Mission 1 – Database Design
Design a small business database containing at least **5 related tables** (for example: customers, products, orders, employees, departments).

---

### Mission 2 – Populate the Database
Insert realistic sample data into every table.

Aim for:
- 15+ customers
- 15+ products
- 30+ orders

---

### Mission 3 – Business Reports
Write SQL queries to answer questions such as:

- Total orders by customer
- Best-selling products
- Average product price
- Customers with no orders
- Highest-value orders
- Total sales by product

---

### Mission 4 – Data Management
Write SQL statements to:

- Update existing records
- Delete unwanted records
- Retrieve filtered data
- Sort results
- Group data

---

### Mission 5 – Final Challenge
Create a small SQL portfolio by organising your work into:

- `schema.sql`
- `insert_data.sql`
- `queries.sql`
- `README.md`

Document:
- Database purpose
- Tables
- Relationships
- Key queries
- Insights discovered

---

## 🏆 Bonus Challenge

Create at least one **VIEW** that simplifies a frequently used business report.

Example ideas:
- Customer Order Summary
- Product Sales Report
- Employee Department Report
